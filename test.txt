### Campaign Goals for JobAxle

* **Increase Brand Awareness** – Position JobAxle as a trusted career and recruitment platform.
* **Generate Quality Leads** – Attract job seekers and employers to the platform.
* **Boost Engagement** – Drive registrations, applications, and platform activity.
* **Build Trust** – Highlight success stories and valuable hiring connections.
* **Drive Conversions** – Turn visitors into active users and hiring partners.
* **Strengthen Retention** – Keep users engaged with relevant opportunities and updates.







# JobAxle Campaign Strategy

## Campaign Title

**JobAxle: Connecting Talent with Opportunity**

## Campaign Objective

Increase brand awareness, attract quality job seekers and employers, boost platform engagement, and drive user registrations.

## Target Audience

### Primary Audience

* Job seekers (students, fresh graduates, and professionals)
* Employers, recruiters, and HR teams

### Secondary Audience

* Internship seekers and early-career talent
* Skilled professionals exploring new opportunities

## Key Message

**"Your next opportunity starts with JobAxle."**

JobAxle helps job seekers discover meaningful career opportunities while enabling employers to find the right talent efficiently.

Strategy Breakdown
1. Full Campaign Strategy → full_campaign
Role: Master Campaign Architect
Logic: Generates a comprehensive omnichannel campaign — Big Idea, SMART objectives, customer journey mapping across Email/Social/Paid/Content, budget allocation %, KPIs, 30-60-90 day timeline, risk mitigation
Output: Sections-based (heading + body + items)


2. Marketing Campaign Ideas → campaign_idea
Role: Head of Creative Strategy
Logic: Identifies a "Cultural Tension" the brand uniquely solves → campaign theme with viral potential → narrative arc (beginning→tension→climax→resolution) → 3-5 emotional messaging pillars → 2-3 radical activation/stunt ideas → 30-60-90 rollout
Output: Sections-based


3. Brand Storytelling Concepts → brand_story
Role: Chief Brand Storyteller
Logic: Identifies a Brand Archetype (Hero, Magician, Outlaw, Sage...) → defines "Enemy" the brand fights against → writes origin story (struggle→revelation→triumph) → composes brand manifesto (100-150 word rallying cry) → maps 3-5 emotional hooks tied to audience fears/desires
Output: Sections-based


4. Social Media Content Strategy → social_strategy
Role: Social Media Creative Director & Algorithm Hacker
Logic: Defines 4-5 hyper-specific content pillars → builds Hero-Hub-Hygiene content pyramid → algorithm-hacking playbook per platform (Saves/Shares on IG, Watch Time on TikTok) → real-time engagement tactics → aggressive 30/60/90 growth targets
Output: Sections-based


5. Ad Copy Variations → ad_copy
Role: Elite Direct-Response Copywriter
Logic: Uses AIDA or PAS framework → 5 pattern-interrupting headline options → 3 body copy variants (Awareness/Consideration/Conversion funnel stages) → 3 CTAs using FOMO/curiosity triggers → 2-3 aggressive hooks → A/B testing hypotheses grounded in behavioral psychology
Output: Rich JSON — headlines[], body_copy[{variant, copy}], ctas[], hooks[], ab_testing_hypotheses[]
6. Product Launch Ideas → product_launch
Role: Launch Strategist & Hype Architect
Logic: Defines "North Star" positioning → builds FOMO mechanics sequence → "Velvet Rope" exclusivity tactics (gamified waitlists, VIP early access) → radical PR angles → influencer/ambassador activation → timeline with psychological urgency triggers
Output: Sections-based
7. Taglines & Slogans → tagline
Role: Naming & Tagline Maestro
Logic: Generates 12-15 tagline options (phonetic flow, double meanings, rhythmic symmetry) → grouped by angles (Emotional, Functional, Challenger, Poetic) → top 3 get rationale explaining the psychological trigger → max 7 words (target 3-4)
Output: Rich JSON — taglines[{tagline, angle, rationale}], top_picks[{tagline, rationale}]
8. Creative Angles → creative_angles
Role: Creative Angle Futurist & Concept Disruptor
Logic: Generates 5-7 radically different creative angles → forces at least one polarizing/counter-intuitive ("zag" when others "zig") → each stems from an unspoken human truth → details execution, differentiation, and best channel
Output: Rich JSON — angles[{name, insight, execution, differentiation, best_channel}]
9. Image Generation Prompt → image_prompt
Role: Visual Creative Director & AI Prompt Engineer
Logic: Generates a hyper-detailed master prompt for Midjourney v6 / DALL-E 3 with extreme technical jargon (camera lenses like 35mm f/1.4, film stock like Kodak Portra 400, lighting like chiaroscuro/volumetric, render engines like Unreal Engine 5) → 3 style variations with different art directions → negative prompts for purity
Output: Rich JSON — master_prompt, style_variations[{name, prompt, negative_prompt}], aspect_ratio
10. Logo Design Brief → logo_design
Role: Elite Brand Identity Designer
Logic: Rooted in Semiotics (study of signs/symbols) and Color Psychology → style recommendation → color palette with subconscious communication rationale → typography pairings with psychological weight → visual metaphors encapsulating the brand's "Why" → 3 distinct logo concepts with vivid descriptions
Output: Rich JSON — sections[] (Style, Color, Typography, Metaphors, Variations, Mood Board), logo_concepts[{name, description, style, color_palette}]
11. Social Media Post → post_content / social_post
Role: Elite Social Content Specialist & Algorithm Hacker
Logic: Generates platform-native posts — per-platform rules (Facebook: storytelling, LinkedIn: contrarian, TikTok: Gen-Z raw, Twitter: zero fluff, YouTube: SEO curiosity gap) → pattern-interrupting hook → frictionless CTA → algorithmic hacks → no emoji spam
Output: Rich JSON — platforms[{platform, hook, body, cta, hashtags[], visual_description, best_time, engagement_tactic}]
12. Brand Identity Guide → brand_identity
Role: Master Brand Identity Architect
Logic: Defines Brand Essence + 3-5 uncompromising Core Values → creates "Anti-Persona" section (who the brand is NOT, what it stands AGAINST) → brand personality traits → sensory branding (how it sounds/feels) → visual identity rules → Tone of Voice with "Do This, Not That" examples
Output: Sections-based
13. Content Calendar → content_calendar
Role: Elite Editorial Strategist
Logic: Structures 4 weekly macro-themes → Content Repurposing Funnel (1 Hero piece → 10+ micro-pieces) → daily breakdown balancing value/entertainment/conversion → engagement tactics and cross-platform synergy → cultural moments/trend-jacking opportunities → production deadlines
Output: Rich JSON — weekly_themes[{week, theme, focus}], daily_breakdown[{day, platform, post_type, topic, engagement_tactic}], special_dates[], content_format_mix{percentages}, repurposing_suggestions[], production_deadlines[]
