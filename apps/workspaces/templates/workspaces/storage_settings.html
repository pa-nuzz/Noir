How storage works

Where your files live

  • Local: Files are saved to the Intelligent Digital Automation server's disk under media/. Works out of the box, no setup.
  • DIA Managed S3: Files are saved to Intelligent Digital Automation's secure S3-compatible cloud storage. Billed at Rs 10/GB/month.
  • Google Drive: Intelligent Digital Automation creates a "DAI" folder in your Drive. You can browse and share those files like any other Drive file.
  • S3-Compatible: Point at your own S3, R2, or MinIO bucket. Intelligent Digital Automation uploads there directly. If you set a CDN/public base URL, asset URLs are public; otherwise they're served via short-lived presigned links.
