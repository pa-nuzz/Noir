How It Works

1

Connect Your Inbox

Add your Gmail or Outlook email address. The AI agent monitors your inbox for new messages.

2

AI Analyzes Incoming Emails

Each message is analyzed for intent, urgency, and sentiment. Threads are summarized automatically.

3

Draft Replies Generated

The AI drafts context-aware replies. Review, edit, and approve drafts before sending.

4

Review & Send

Approve or edit drafts from the dashboard. Approved drafts are sent through your connected SMTP sender.
